Short bio

Joshua Ferguson is the founder of VeriSwarm, where he's building the infrastructure that lets regulated industries deploy AI agents — identity, policy enforcement, audit trails, and verifiable evidence. He came to AI governance from a background in infrastructure engineering, running production workloads across global data centers.

Extended bio

Joshua Ferguson founded VeriSwarm after years as an Infrastructure Architect, where he managed workloads across multiple global data centers and built Kubernetes-native platforms with Terraform. He saw the same governance problems that plague traditional infrastructure becoming critical in AI — agents making decisions with no audit trail, no identity, and no way to prove what happened. VeriSwarm is his answer: the missing infrastructure layer between AI capabilities and real-world deployment in finance, healthcare, and government.
