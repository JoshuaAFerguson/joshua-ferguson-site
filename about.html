About

Joshua Ferguson

Joshua Ferguson is the founder of VeriSwarm and a systems and infrastructure engineer.

Short Bio

Joshua Ferguson is the founder of VeriSwarm, where he is building trust and security infrastructure for AI agents. His work is focused on the controls organizations need before autonomous systems can be trusted in production, especially in regulated environments.

Current Work

At VeriSwarm, his focus is on identity, policy enforcement, audit trails, and verifiable evidence for AI systems. The premise is simple: if an organization cannot inspect, govern, and prove what an agent did, it cannot responsibly deploy that agent in a high-trust environment.

Background

Before founding VeriSwarm, Joshua worked as an Infrastructure Architect running workloads across multiple global data centers. He has built Kubernetes-native platforms, worked extensively with Terraform, and shipped infrastructure into environments where downtime and control failures carry real operational and regulatory consequences.

Public Profile

He also maintains a public speaking profile on Sessionize, writes about AI systems and ethics, and publishes technical work and curriculum through GitHub.
